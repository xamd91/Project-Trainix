Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, attending, and managing training sessions.

Booking

How do I book a training session?

Browse the Training Catalogue and click "View Details" on any session you're interested in. From the details page you can submit a booking request. Your manager will be notified and must approve the request before your place is confirmed.

Do I need manager approval to book?

Yes. All booking requests are routed to your line manager for approval. You'll receive an email notification once your booking has been confirmed or declined. This ensures your training aligns with your team's schedule.

Can I cancel or reschedule a booking?

You can cancel a confirmed booking from your Account page up to 48 hours before the session starts. Rescheduling is not available directly — please cancel and re-book the preferred session. Cancellations inside 48 hours require contacting the trainer directly.

What happens if a session is fully booked?

If a session is at capacity the booking button will show as "Fully Booked". Check back regularly as spaces may become available if other learners cancel, or look out for repeat sessions in the catalogue.

Attending

How do I join an online session?

Once your booking is confirmed you'll receive a joining link by email. The link will also be available in your Account page under "Upcoming Sessions". We recommend joining 5 minutes early to check your audio and camera.

What do I need for an in-person session?

Your confirmation email will include the venue address and any materials to bring. Please arrive at least 10 minutes early. Attendance is recorded by the trainer at the start of each session.

Will I receive a certificate after completing a session?

Certificates of completion are issued for eligible courses. Where applicable, your certificate will appear in your Account page within 2 working days of the session date, once attendance has been confirmed by the trainer.

Account

How do I register for an account?

Click the Register button in the navigation bar and complete the sign-up form using your company email address. Your account will be active immediately and you can start browsing sessions straight away.

I've forgotten my password. What do I do?

On the Login page, click "Forgot password?" and enter your registered email address. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or contact us using the form below.

Where can I see my booking history?

Log in and head to your Account page. There you'll find tabs for Upcoming Sessions, Past Sessions, and any certificates you've earned.

Managers

How do I approve a booking request?

You'll receive an email notification when a team member submits a booking request. Log in and visit your Manager Dashboard to review, approve, or decline pending requests. You can also add a note when declining.

Can I view training history for my team?

Yes. Your Manager Dashboard includes an overview of all past and upcoming sessions for each member of your team, along with attendance records and completion status.
